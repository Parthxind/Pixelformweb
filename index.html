Pixelform Business Rules & Pricing Guidelines

Quotes Valid: 7 Days UPI: 7024980813-2@ybl
Rule 01 // Scope & Approval Written Quote First

Work starts only after the client approves a written quote that lists the exact scope. Anything outside the quote is billed as a fixed add-on or customisation.

Rule 02 // Communication WhatsApp Business Primary

WhatsApp Business is the main channel for briefs, approvals, and delivery. An approval given in chat counts as official written approval.

Rule 03 // Timelines Brief & Material Dependent

Delivery time starts once the client's brief and materials are complete. If the client delays feedback or materials, the delivery date moves by the same amount.

Rule 04 // Revisions 1 Included Revision Round

Each service includes the revision rounds stated in its base price (1 round). A revision means changes to existing work; a new direction counts as customisation. Extra rounds: ₹750/round.

Rule 05 // Client Materials Assets & Rights

Clients supply their own text, photos, logos, and account access unless an add-on covers them. Clients are responsible for having rights to everything they supply.

Rule 06 // Ownership & Handover Full Payment Before Handover

Final files and usage rights pass to the client only once the project is fully paid. Pixelform may showcase finished work in its portfolio unless the client opts out in writing.

Rule 07 // Confidentiality Strict Privacy

Client files, logins, and business details stay strictly private. They are never shared or reused for other clients.

Rule 08 // Instagram & Quotes Collaborator Access & 7-Day Validity

Clients always own their IG account; Pixelform works via collaborator access and makes no guarantee of followers, reach, or sales. All quotes are valid for 7 days.
