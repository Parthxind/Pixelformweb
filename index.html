Studio Policies & Terms

16 transparent studio rules that protect every project

Clear expectations from day one ensure smooth creative momentum, predictable milestones, and full legal ownership on final handover.

Rule 01

50 / 50 Payment Split

Projects begin once a 50% booking advance clears. The remaining 50% balance is due upon final design approval before releasing editable/print files or launching live web builds.

Rule 02

Structured Revision Rounds

Each package includes clearly specified revision rounds (2 to 3 rounds). Consolidating feedback into one list per round keeps timelines sharp and avoids extra revision charges.

Rule 03

Commercial Ownership & Source Handover

Upon 100% payment completion, full commercial usage rights and final source deliverables (AI, SVG, CMYK PDFs, or clean web code) transfer to the client.

Rule 04

Client-Owned Domain & Hosting

Website domains, SSL certificates, and hosting plans are registered in the client's name and billing account so you retain permanent independent control.

Rule 05

Turnaround & Feedback Windows

Estimated delivery timelines assume feedback within 48 hours of each milestone presentation. Delayed client approvals shift final delivery dates proportionally.

Rule 06

Cancellation & Rush Terms

The 50% booking advance secures dedicated studio capacity and is non-refundable once concept exploration begins. Rush turnarounds require prior slot confirmation.

Rule 07

Client Copy, Legal Text & Content Readiness

Clients supply final written copy, product weights, barcodes, FSSAI/regulatory text, and brand photos before production begins. Proofreading legal compliance copy remains the client's responsibility.

Rule 08

Print Production & Color Proofing

We deliver calibrated CMYK print-ready files with accurate bleeds. Because paper stocks and ink calibration vary across print vendors, we recommend requesting a physical wet proof before bulk printing.

Rule 09

1-Month Post-Launch Web Support Window

Every base website design includes 1 month of complimentary error fixation and minor post-launch revisions covering layout bugs, broken links, or form checks.

Rule 10

Web Care & Maintenance Billing Terms

Ongoing Web Care plans (monthly or yearly) are billed in advance and cover routine updates, monitoring, and maintenance. Full redesigns or new custom modules are quoted separately.

Rule 11

Font, Stock & Third-Party Licensing

We prioritize open commercial fonts and original artwork. If a project requires paid commercial fonts, premium stock photography, or third-party plugins, licenses are billed directly to the client.

Rule 12

Project Pause & Reactivation Policy

Projects inactive without client response for more than 21 consecutive days enter paused status. Reactivating a paused project requires re-scheduling into the next available studio slot.

Rule 13

Mid-Project Scope Additions

New pages, extra packaging SKUs, or direction pivots requested after initial concept sign-off are documented and quoted cleanly as add-ons before additional work starts.

Rule 14

Portfolio Showcase & NDA Confidentiality

Pixelform reserves the right to showcase completed work in our portfolio after your public launch. Need strict confidentiality? Request an NDA before kickoff and we will keep your project private.

Rule 15

Regional Currency & Quote Validity

All issued studio quotations remain valid for 14 calendar days from the quote date and are invoiced in the client's verified billing currency.

Rule 16

Final File Backup & Archiving Window

We archive delivered master project files securely for 90 days after final handover. Please download and store your delivered package promptly upon receipt.
